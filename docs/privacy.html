# Web Purge Privacy Policy

## Privacy Policy for Web Purge

Effective Date: [Insert Date]

Web Purge is a privacy utility browser extension designed to enhance your online privacy by removing all browser data associated with configured target websites. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to your privacy and explains how we handle your data.

### 1. Data Collection

Web Purge does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All operations are performed locally within your browser, and no external network requests are made. We do not track your browsing activity or maintain any logs of your usage.

### 2. Data Removal

The extension allows you to remove the following types of browser data associated with specified domains:

- Browsing history (URLs)
- Cookies
- HTTP cache
- Site storage (localStorage, IndexedDB, CacheStorage, Service Workers)

### 3. User Control

You have full control over the data removal process. The extension provides options to configure which domains to target, and you can manually trigger data removal at any time. Additionally, you can enable or disable categories of domains as per your preference.

### 4. No Telemetry

Web Purge does not include any telemetry features. We do not collect any usage statistics or analytics data. Your privacy is our priority, and we respect your right to use the extension without being monitored.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
